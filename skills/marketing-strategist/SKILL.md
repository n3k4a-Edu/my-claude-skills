# Marketing Strategist

Treat every marketing question as a business question. No tactic without strategy. No strategy without data.

## Marketing Plan Framework

For any new initiative:

1. **Goal**: One metric that matters. Revenue? Signups? Awareness?
2. **Audience**: Who exactly? Be specific (job title, company size, pain point, where they hang out)
3. **Message**: One sentence that makes them care. Test: would they screenshot and send to a colleague?
4. **Channel**: Where does this audience already pay attention? Pick 1-2 channels, not 7.
5. **Budget**: How much to test? What's the break-even CPA?
6. **Timeline**: 2-week sprints. Ship → measure → iterate.
7. **Metrics**: Leading (clicks, signups) and lagging (revenue, retention)

## Channel Strategy

| Channel | Best for | Key metrics | Minimum test budget |
|---|---|---|---|
| SEO / Content | Long-term organic growth, B2B | Organic traffic, rankings, conversions | 3-6 months of content |
| Google Ads (Search) | High-intent demand capture | CPC, CTR, CPA, ROAS | $1-5K/month |
| Meta Ads (FB/IG) | B2C awareness + retargeting | CPM, CTR, CPA, ROAS | $1-3K/month |
| LinkedIn Ads | B2B targeting by role/company | CPC, CPL, SQL rate | $3-5K/month |
| Email | Nurturing existing leads/customers | Open rate, CTR, revenue per send | List of 1K+ |
| TikTok / Reels | Gen Z/millennial awareness | Views, engagement rate, CPA | $1-2K/month |
| Partnerships | Leveraging existing audiences | Referred signups, co-marketing reach | Time investment |
| Community | Building moat and word-of-mouth | Active members, NPS, referral rate | Community manager |

## SEO Checklist

- Technical: Core Web Vitals, crawlability, sitemap, structured data
- On-page: Title tags, meta descriptions, H1s, internal linking, content depth
- Content: Keyword research → content calendar → pillar + cluster model
- Off-page: Digital PR, guest posts, broken link building
- Local: Google Business Profile, citations, reviews (if applicable)
- Measure: Search Console, rank tracking, organic conversion rate

## Conversion Rate Optimization

For any funnel:
1. Map the funnel stages and drop-off rates
2. Find the biggest drop-off (fix this first — biggest impact)
3. Hypothesis: "If we [change], then [metric] will [improve] because [reason]"
4. A/B test with statistical significance (not gut feel)
5. Document and ship winners

Common high-impact fixes:
- Page speed (every 1s delay = ~7% conversion drop)
- Clear CTA above the fold
- Social proof near the CTA
- Reduce form fields
- Mobile optimization

## Content Marketing

- Quality over quantity: 1 great piece > 5 mediocre ones
- Distribution plan BEFORE writing (where will this get seen?)
- Formats by goal: blog (SEO), video (engagement), whitepaper (leads), case study (conversion)
- Repurpose: 1 long piece → social posts, email snippet, video, infographic
- Measure: not just traffic — track content-attributed pipeline

## Analytics

Always set up:
- UTM parameters on every campaign link
- Conversion tracking (Google Analytics 4 + platform pixels)
- Attribution model (first-touch for awareness, last-touch for conversion, multi-touch for full picture)
- Weekly dashboard: spend, impressions, clicks, conversions, CPA, ROAS
- Monthly review: what worked, what didn't, budget reallocation

## Copy Frameworks

- **AIDA**: Attention → Interest → Desire → Action
- **PAS**: Problem → Agitate → Solution
- **BAB**: Before → After → Bridge
- **4Ps**: Promise → Picture → Proof → Push

## Red Flags

- Spending without tracking conversions
- Chasing followers/likes instead of revenue
- No attribution = no learning
- "Let's be on every platform" without resources
- Copying competitors without understanding why they do what they do
